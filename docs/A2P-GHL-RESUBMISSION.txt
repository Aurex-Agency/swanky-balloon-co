CAMPAIGN DESCRIPTION — paste into the description field
Swanky Balloon Co. LLC provides balloon decor and event styling in Middle Tennessee. We send texts to people who submit an inquiry at https://swanky-balloon-co.vercel.app/inquire and explicitly select the applicable SMS consent checkbox. Inquiry texts include replies to questions, quotes, booking confirmations, reminders and event-day delivery or pickup updates. Separately opted-in marketing subscribers receive recurring special offers, seasonal balloon decor announcements and service promotions. Each message category requires its own optional checkbox; choosing one does not authorize the other. Message frequency varies. Recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

SAMPLE MESSAGE 1 — inquiry/customer care
Swanky Balloon Co. LLC: Hi [First Name], thanks for your balloon decor inquiry for [Event Date]. What time can we access the venue for setup? Reply STOP to opt out.

SAMPLE MESSAGE 2 — promotional
Swanky Balloon Co. LLC: Planning your next celebration? Explore our balloon decor services at https://swanky-balloon-co.vercel.app/services and request a custom quote. Reply STOP to opt out.

ADDITIONAL SAMPLE — booking reminder, if another field is available
Swanky Balloon Co. LLC: Hi [First Name], this is a reminder of your balloon installation on [Event Date]. Our arrival window is [Arrival Window]. Reply with any access updates. Reply STOP to opt out.

HOW CONTACTS OPT IN / MESSAGE FLOW — paste into that field
Customers opt in through our public website inquiry form at https://swanky-balloon-co.vercel.app/inquire. They complete The Event, The Vision and The Investment, then enter their mobile number in step 4, Your Details. They independently select “Text me about my inquiry (optional)” for inquiry and booking messages and/or “Send me offers and inspiration (optional)” for marketing texts, then click Send My Inquiry. Both SMS checkboxes start unchecked and are optional. Customers can select either, both or neither; entering a number or accepting the general inquiry terms alone does not subscribe them to SMS. Each checkbox identifies Swanky Balloon Co. LLC, the message categories, variable frequency, message/data rates, HELP and STOP instructions, and that consent is not a condition of purchase. Privacy Policy and Terms links appear beside the checkboxes. The same disclosures and step-by-step instructions are publicly readable at https://swanky-balloon-co.vercel.app/inquire#text-message-choices. Our website submission includes the separate consent choices, exact accepted disclosure text/version and submission timestamp. Privacy Policy: https://swanky-balloon-co.vercel.app/privacy. Text message terms: https://swanky-balloon-co.vercel.app/terms#text-messages.

This draft describes website opt-in only. If QR codes, paper forms, phone scripts, chat widgets or text-to-join are also used, provide their actual disclosures and evidence so the flow can be expanded accurately. Do not claim keyword or verbal enrollment without an implemented, documented process.
