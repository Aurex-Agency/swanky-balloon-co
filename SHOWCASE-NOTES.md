# A 60-second Swanky walkthrough

**0–12 seconds: The first impression.** Open http://127.0.0.1:3000. Show the refined serif typography, ivory and dusty blue palette, and Swanky’s three confirmed installations. The design follows the owner’s supplied brand boards.

**12–23 seconds: Make it personal.** Scroll to The Color Studio. Try Soft Bloom, remove a color, choose a new one and select an occasion. Click Use This Palette.

**23–35 seconds: A better inquiry.** Point out the carried-over palette. Enter a date and venue city. Continue to the vision step and choose services. The customer gets a clear path without needing to know every detail upfront.

**35–43 seconds: Structured from the start.** Explain that the form captures event, creative, budget, contact and campaign details as separate fields. A configured GoHighLevel webhook can create the contact, opportunity, tags and follow-up tasks. Without that connection, submission is a clearly labeled demo and is not sent to Swanky.

**43–55 seconds: The business side.** Open http://127.0.0.1:3000/ops-preview directly. Show the sample pipeline, inquiry detail, proposed automation sequence and event calendar. This is static sample data, not a live CRM.

**55–60 seconds: The proposal.** Aurex is proposing a complete lead-to-event operating system: a stronger brand experience, clearer inquiries, consistent follow-up and an organized path to event day.

## Before the client sees it

Start `npm run dev -- --hostname 127.0.0.1`, keep `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_MODE=true`, and use demo contact details. The site uses no fabricated testimonials, service prices or client names. The CRM figures are explicitly labeled sample data. There are no em dashes in the authored site copy.
