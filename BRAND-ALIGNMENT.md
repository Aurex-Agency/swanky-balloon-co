# Swanky brand alignment

Source: [Owner’s Google Drive brand and inspiration folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1yQysde3pSm6hC9DVXlXlkInEsVtj8wpj?usp=sharing), reviewed September 24, 2026. All twelve numbered boards were reviewed.

## Visual direction

Boards 1–5 favor ivory, dusty blue, sage, taupe and chocolate; classic serif wordmarks; an SBC monogram; and subtle striped or textured surfaces. The website uses these cues in its wordmark, headings, restrained borders, pinstripes, color studio and inquiry form. The prior neon sticker treatment is no longer displayed.

No font names, editable logo files or numerical color specifications were supplied. Cormorant Garamond is a locally bundled serif interpretation of the references, with Manrope for controls and supporting text. The typographic wordmark and monogram are web adaptations, not imported master logos. Color values are documented in `src/data/swanky.ts` and CSS tokens in `src/app/globals.css`.

## Approved photography

The user explicitly confirmed all three freestanding installations on board 8 as Swanky’s own work and approved them for the website. Each panel was extracted and optimized as WebP without generative edits:

- Pink and ivory garland beside a barn door.
- Pink and lilac pillars in an indoor venue.
- Green balloon installation beside a column.

Images live in `public/swanky/portfolio/`. Their supplied board resolution limits available detail. Descriptions and filters identify visible palettes, without inventing an event occasion. Full images are available in the lightbox.

## Services and copy

Board 11 supplies the pickup and delivery menu. The website includes all six pickup options and eleven delivery options, grouped into four inquiry categories. Backdrops, marquee lights and easels are described as balloon installations around those pieces; their rental or inclusion is not promised. Pricing, pickup logistics and final scope are confirmed in the proposal.

Copy is calmer and more personal, with plain descriptions of garlands, helium arrangements and custom installations. No em dashes are used. Color preferences carry from the homepage into the inquiry form. Draft storage uses version 2 so obsolete service names and palettes cannot be restored from version 1.

Boards 6, 7, 9, 10 and 12 include third-party inspiration. Their logos, portraits, pricing, packaging and imagery have not been used as Swanky work. A founding date visible in a mockup is not treated as a verified business fact. No owner portrait, customer testimonials or additional services have been invented.

## Remaining launch inputs

The existing live integration, privacy copy, response-time estimate and contact details still need final owner setup or confirmation. The brand update does not enable inquiry delivery or publish a Vercel deployment.

## Homepage creative refinement

The homepage was subsequently redesigned to restore more visual energy within the same brand system. The opening uses a dusty blue field, oversized serif typography, an arched photograph and a manual selector for all three approved installations. Services use an editorial list beside a photographed installation, with pickup and delivery menus in accessible disclosures. The color studio sits on a chocolate background; the closing invitation uses slate. Repeated supporting sections were removed. Desktop, tablet and phone layouts preserve the inquiry and gallery flows, keyboard controls and reduced-motion preferences.
